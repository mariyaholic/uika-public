# 04 — Motion

Motion is the explanation, not the garnish. If a transform does not teach, cut it.

## Pacing
- A beat is 5–15 s; hold the finished frame ≥ 1.5 s before the next beat so the eye can settle.
- Build at the speed of comprehension: a reveal is 0.8–1.5 s, a transform 1.2–1.8 s.
- One thing moves at a time. Two simultaneous animations are only allowed when they *are* one idea
  (a point moving while its projection traces).

## What to animate
- A **construction**: a line sweeping to draw a curve; an area filling to make an integral.
- A **transform** that is an argument: discrete sum → integral, table → curve, before → after.
- A **focus shift**: dim everything, colour the one part that matters (`reveal_line`).

## What never to animate
- Text flying in from off-screen with no meaning.
- Camera flourishes, spins, or zooms that do not reveal structure.
- Anything that loops for effect.
- A fade between beats that hides a broken transition. Use a cut or a deliberate transform.

## Cadence
Choose one motion vocabulary and keep it: this pack is **smooth and continuous** (unlike the stepped PC-98
pack). Easing is default manim; avoid `rate_func=there_and_back` except for a deliberate emphasis beat.
